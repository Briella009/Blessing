# AAIO impact and adoption ledger

AAIO separates **project activity** from **external recognition**. This page records only evidence that can be independently checked. It exists to avoid overstating adoption, citation or institutional use.

## What counts as external evidence

Examples include:

- an accepted upstream incident submission;
- an external pull request or named contributor;
- a paper, report or article that cites or uses AAIO;
- a conference or workshop that publicly lists an AAIO presentation;
- documented reuse of the dataset, methodology, schema or interoperability export;
- a public institutional reference, media mention or research collaboration;
- a DOI-backed archived release and subsequent citations.

GitHub stars, page views and self-authored social posts may be useful engagement signals, but they are not treated here as independent evidence that AAIO advanced the field.

## Presentations and publications

Only externally published or publicly listed outputs should be added here. Drafts, private talks and planned submissions should remain outside the verified ledger until there is public evidence.

## Maintenance rule

Every new entry should include a stable public URL or other independently checkable evidence. If evidence later disappears or a claim is corrected, retain the historical entry and add a correction note rather than silently deleting the record.
